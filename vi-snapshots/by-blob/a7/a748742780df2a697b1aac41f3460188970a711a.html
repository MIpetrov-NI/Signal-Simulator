Signal Generator.lvlib:Shutdown Generator.vi

Shutdown stage. Finalises a generation run when the client cancels or an error stops the acquisition loop and reports the total number of samples produced. The simulator holds no hardware sessions, files or other resources, so nothing needs to be closed here; this is the single place to release resources if the engine ever acquires any.
