Signal Simulator Plug-In.lvclass:Measurement Logic.vi

Contains the core logic for executing the measurement. Configures and initializes the sine generator, then continuously acquires a window of sine wave data and publishes it to the client with Update Results every update interval until the client cancels or an error occurs. The generator is then shut down and the last window is returned as the final measurement result.
