Signal Generator.lvlib:Initialize Generator.vi

Initialization stage. Starts a new generation run: resets the absolute sample counter to zero so the first block begins at t = 0 with zero phase, and hands the validated generator settings on to the acquisition loop. The simulator uses no hardware, so there are no sessions or resources to open here.
