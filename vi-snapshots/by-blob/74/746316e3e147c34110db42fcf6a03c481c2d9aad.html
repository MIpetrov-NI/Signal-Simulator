Signal Simulator Plug-In UI.lvlib:Measurement UI.vi

Front panel for the Signal Simulator measurement plug-in. Control labels match the elements of Measurement Configuration.ctl and indicator labels match the elements of Measurement Results.ctl, which is how the measurement framework binds the panel to the measurement. The loop services the framework messages Initialize, Post UI Update and Exit that are posted to a queue named after this VI.
