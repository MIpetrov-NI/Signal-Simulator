Sine Generator.lvlib:Initialize Sine Generator.vi

Initialization stage. Starts a new generation run: resets the absolute sample counter to zero so the first block starts with zero phase, and passes the validated settings on to the acquisition loop. The simulator uses no hardware, so there are no sessions or resources to open here.
