Sine Generator.lvlib:Configure Sine Generator.vi

Configuration stage. Sanitises the user settings so the engine never sees an invalid value: amplitude is made non-negative, sample rate is coerced to 1 S/s .. 10 MS/s, frequency to 0 .. Nyquist (sample rate / 2), number of samples to 2 .. 1 000 000 and update interval to 10 .. 10 000 ms. Returns one generator settings cluster.
