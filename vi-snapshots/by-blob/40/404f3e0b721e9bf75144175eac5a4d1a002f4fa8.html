Signal Simulator Plug-In.lvclass:Measurement Logic.vi

Contains the core logic for executing the measurement. Configures and initializes the signal generator, then continuously acquires blocks of synthetic waveform data and publishes each block to the client with Update Results until the client cancels or an error occurs. The generator is shut down and the last block is returned as the final measurement result.
