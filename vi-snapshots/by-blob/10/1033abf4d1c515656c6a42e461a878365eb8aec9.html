Signal Generator.lvlib:Configure Generator.vi

Configuration stage. Sanitises the user-supplied generator settings so the signal engine never receives an invalid value: amplitude and noise level are made non-negative, sample rate is coerced to 1 S/s .. 10 MS/s and frequency to 0 .. Nyquist. It also derives the streaming block size (about 100 ms of data, 16 .. 100000 samples) and the update interval in milliseconds.
